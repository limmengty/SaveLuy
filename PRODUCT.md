# BrokeNoMore Product Context

## Purpose & Premise
BrokeNoMore is a calm daily financial clarity system built to answer one singular question at any moment:
> **"What can I spend today without derailing my rent, bills, or weekly commitments?"**

Unlike retroactive budgeting apps that induce guilt through historical pie charts, BrokeNoMore is prospective and operational:
1. **Prospective daily guidance:** Recomputes your daily safe spending allowance every morning based on elapsed days, spent funds, and remaining flexible limits.
2. **Instant pre-purchase consequence check:** A 10-key calculator panel simulates the ripple effect on your week *before* money leaves your wallet.
3. **Protected commitments stay protected:** Rent, fixed subscriptions, and emergency funds are locked away from daily decision commingling.
4. **Friction-free single-tap capture:** Common small transactions (coffee, transport, meal) take under 1 second to record.

## Target Audience & Locale
- Bilingual Cambodian & international users operating in dual currencies: USD ($) and Khmer Riel (៛).
- Focus on clarity, dignity, and low cognitive load under financial stress.

## Tone & Philosophy
- **Voice:** Calm, grounded, non-judgmental ("Calm & Grounded" or direct "Straight Talk").
- **Zero shame:** No red warning banners for standard spending; instead, clear mathematical adjustments ("next days become about $14.20/day").
- **Exact math:** All monetary values stored and calculated as pure integer minor units (cents) to eliminate IEEE 754 floating-point drift.
